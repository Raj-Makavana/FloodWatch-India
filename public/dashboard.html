Frequently Asked Questions & Guide

Telemetry interpretations, AI model accuracy, automated early warnings & disaster safety protocols

↗️ Open Full Knowledge Base
🔍
📧 How does the Automated Email Alert System trigger? ▼
Whenever meteorological radar data or machine learning flood probability models exceed your configured sensitivity threshold (e.g. 60%), emergency broadcast emails are automatically generated and dispatched to your registered address with live river discharge, forecasted rain, and safety instructions.
🤖 What is the prediction accuracy of the State ML Models? ▼
Each of India's 37 states and UTs has an individual Gradient Boosting Classifier trained on 25+ years of verified NDMA historical flood disasters, IMD rainfall, dam gross storage, and GIS elevation metrics. Cross-validation accuracy ranges from 91.4% to 98.2%.
🎚️ Can I change my alert threshold and monitoring district? ▼
Yes! Use the Edit Profile page to update your preferred state, district, or email threshold (40%, 60%, 75%). You can also toggle auto-dispatch on or off anytime.
🌊 Where does the live river water level and dam telemetry come from? ▼
Telemetry is pulled from Central Water Commission (CWC) gauge telemetry stations, India-WRIS dam reservoir telemetry, and IMD Mausam meteorological rainfall radars, updated every 15-30 minutes.
🚨 What are the official emergency helpline numbers? ▼
NDRF National Helpline: 1078 / 011-24363260
National Emergency: 112
State Disaster Authority (SDMA): 1070
District Disaster Helpline: 1077
🛡️ What precautions should residents take when Risk is HIGH? ▼
Move to upper floors or designated relief centers; disconnect electric mains; avoid wading through moving floodwaters; keep mobile phones charged and battery backup torches on hand; keep essential documents in waterproof bags.
