Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our machine learning flood risk prediction algorithms, automated early warnings, meteorological radar, and citizen safety protocols.

🔍
What is FloodWatch India and what is its primary objective? GENERAL
▼
FloodWatch India is an advanced AI-powered meteorological intelligence platform dedicated to real-time inundation risk forecasting and automated early disaster warnings across India. It ingests telemetry from the Central Water Commission (CWC), India Meteorological Department (IMD) Mausam forecasts, GIS elevation grids, and over 25 years of documented historical flood disasters to empower disaster responders and citizens with proactive warnings before riverbanks breach.
How does the AI flood risk prediction model work? AI MODEL
▼
Rather than relying on generic nationwide approximations, FloodWatch India deploys 37 specialized Gradient Boosting Classifiers (one dedicated ML model per state and Union Territory). Each state model is trained on multi-factor variables:
  • 24-hour and 72-hour cumulative precipitation (mm)
  • Catchment soil moisture saturation index
  • Upstream dam gross storage capacity and live spillway discharge (m³/s)
  • Catchment elevation gradients & historical DFSI (District Flood Severity Index)
  • Population vulnerability in low-lying river plains
How accurate are the state-wise flood risk predictions? ACCURACY
▼
Based on independent cross-validation against official National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) incident reports from 2000 to 2026, our state models demonstrate an overall cross-validation accuracy ranging from 91.4% to 98.2%. You can review the complete state-wise accuracy metrics directly in the Command Center under the "ML Risk Predictor" module.
How does the Automated Email Alert System work? EMAIL ALERTS
▼
Whenever our continuous sensor polling engine detects that calculated flood probability for your monitored jurisdiction crosses your chosen sensitivity threshold (e.g. 60%), our SMTP alert engine immediately generates an emergency broadcast email. The email includes catchment location, severity badge, forecasted rainfall, river flow volume, IMD meteorological bulletins, and emergency contact helplines.
Can I customize the risk sensitivity threshold for receiving email alerts? PREFERENCES
▼
Yes! You can configure your alert threshold to 40% (Medium Risk Advisory), 60% (High Inundation Warning), or 75% (Critical Disaster Alert). Simply visit your Edit Profile page or the National Alerts tab in the Command Center to save your preference.
What do the Risk Levels (Normal, Medium, High, Extreme) signify? RISK MATRIX
▼
  • 🟢 Normal (0 - 39%): Catchments and river gauges within safe non-flood levels. Routine telemetry monitoring active.
  • 🟡 Medium Risk (40 - 59%): Elevated runoff and localized waterlogging possible. Residents in low-lying riparian tracts should stay watchful.
  • 🟠 High Risk (60 - 74%): River discharge approaching or exceeding danger marks. Bank breaches possible; preparatory relief measures recommended.
  • 🔴 Extreme Hazard (75% - 100%): Severe inundation emergency. Catchment overflowing; immediate evacuation to identified relief shelters mandated.
How does the "My Location" GPS risk detection work? GPS SENSORS
▼
When you click "📍 My Location", your browser coordinates (latitude & longitude) are reverse-geocoded against our GIS district boundary repository. The system instantly queries the latest meteorological conditions, nearest CWC river gauge discharge, and live model probability for your exact coordinates.
What essential actions should I take during a flood warning? SAFETY TIPS
▼
  1. Immediately move to higher ground or upper storeys of pucca concrete structures.
  2. Disconnect all main electrical circuit breakers and gas cylinders.
  3. Do not attempt to walk, swim, or drive through moving floodwaters (even 6 inches of rapid water can knock a person over).
  4. Keep essential emergency kit ready: potable water, non-perishable food, torch with spare batteries, first-aid, waterproof pouch for Aadhaar/deeds.
  5. Monitor official FloodWatch India advisories and listen to district administration loudspeaker alerts.
What are the key national disaster helpline numbers in India? HELPLINES
▼
Save these toll-free round-the-clock emergency numbers:
  • National Disaster Response Force (NDRF): 1078 / 011-24363260
  • All-India Emergency Number: 112
  • State Disaster Management Authority (SDMA): 1070
  • District Disaster Emergency Control Room: 1077
  • Ambulance / Medical Emergency: 108
How do I update my profile, preferred state, or change my password? ACCOUNT
▼
Head over to the Edit Profile page from the top navigation bar. You can choose a custom avatar emoji, update your contact details, set your organization role, select your primary state and district, or update your security password.
Is FloodWatch India free for citizen use? FREE ACCESS
▼
Yes! FloodWatch India is fully open and free for all Indian residents, students, researchers, and government relief personnel. All live interactive maps, district predictions, and automated email warning subscriptions are provided without any subscription charges.
How frequently is rainfall radar and river gauge telemetry updated? TELEMETRY REFRESH
▼
Sensor data from our Neon database and external IMD/CWC telemetry feeds are refreshed automatically every 15 to 30 minutes during active monsoon depressions and cyclone warnings.
